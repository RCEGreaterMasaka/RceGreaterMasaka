Established in 2016 and officially hosted by Uganda Martyrs University Nkozi, RCE Greater Masaka is a prestigious United Nations University (UNU-IAS) acknowledged Regional Centre of Expertise dedicated to advancing Education for Sustainable Development.

Spanning 13 districts across the South-Eastern Lake Victoria Crescent — including Bukomansimbi, Butambala, Gomba, Kalangala, Kalungu, Kyotera, Lwengo, Lyantonde, Masaka, Mityana, Mpigi, Rakai, and Ssembabule — we bridge academic excellence with community action.

Through collaboration with NEMA, Nature Uganda, SIDA, UMUWEC and other partners, we are spearheading Education for Sustainable Development in this region of the Pearl of Africa.
